Our story

Why we play.

Sue's Angels FC was founded and led by Stephen Epathite in 2025, built around a single idea: that a football club can be a way of keeping someone's memory alive.

The club carries the name of Susan Anne Martin. After Sue died of sepsis in 2020, the people closest to her wanted her name to stay part of something good, week after week, season after season.

So we play. Every match is raised in her memory, and every season the club raises awareness of sepsis through charity games and community work, keeping Sue at the heart of everything we do.

We are a football family before we are a football team, bound by respect, resilience, and the standard we hold each other to every Sunday. Champions in year one; hungry for what comes next.
