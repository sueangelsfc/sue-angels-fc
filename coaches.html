The backroom · 26/27

The coaches.

Behind an unbeaten league season sits a management team that sets the standard. Meet the people who built the culture, picked the sides and drove the Angels to the League Ten title.

Management team

Founder

First-team manager

Stephen Epathite

Founder & manager · Fulham F.C. supporter

Stephen Epathite brings decades of experience and leadership to the game, both on and off the pitch. As a player he represented respected non-league sides including Corinthian-Casuals, Chessington & Hook United and Uxbridge, building a deep understanding of the semi-professional and grassroots landscape.

His managerial journey has taken in both the men's and women's game, and it is his eye for player development, his standards and his belief in people that shaped Sue's Angels from the very first day. He founded the club in memory of Susan Anne Martin, and led it to the League Ten title, unbeaten, at the first attempt.

First-team coach

Louis Allen

Organisation & matchday preparation

Louis Allen was a constant presence in the Sue's Angels backroom throughout the 25/26 season, a campaign that ended with the League Ten title and an unbeaten record. His contribution goes far beyond the ninety minutes, from organisation and matchday preparation to the small details that keep a squad ready.

It is his character, though, that sets him apart. A positive, dependable figure who lifts the group and holds everyone to the same standards, Louis embodies the togetherness and culture that have driven the club forward. A valued member of staff and a key part of the journey.
