# UK grassroots funder map

A starting map of funders that pay grassroots/amateur sports clubs. **Always verify current amounts, deadlines and eligibility on the funder's own site before recommending — these change.** Amounts below are indicative, not guarantees.

## Football-specific
- **Football Foundation** (footballfoundation.org.uk) — the big one. Several funds:
  - *Goalpost Fund* — up to ~£1,200 per pair (part-cost) for adult/youth goalposts. Rolling. Low effort. Open to any affiliated club.
  - *Grass Pitch Maintenance Fund* / *Pitch Preparation Fund* — equipment/maintenance for pitches a club maintains.
  - *Small Grants / Facilities (via county FA)* — up to ~£25k, 75% of cost, match funding required. Higher effort. Facilities, storage, changing rooms.
  - *Lionesses HERe to Play* and similar — **women's/girls' teams only.** Do not recommend to a men's/boys' club.
  - Requires a free Football Foundation portal account + county FA affiliation number.
- **The FA / County FA grassroots funds** — county FAs (London FA, Surrey FA, etc.) run small grants and run-a-club support. Confirm which county FA the club is affiliated to first.
- **Sport England — Movement Fund** (sportengland.org) — £300–£15k. Rolling, apply ~12 weeks ahead. Targets inactivity and inequalities in physical activity (not gender-specific — frame around whichever inactive group the club serves). Under £10k avoids the heavier Tier 1 governance requirement; needs 3 unrelated committee members with equal votes.

## Community / cause (often the best fit — usually NOT gender-restricted)
- **National Lottery — Awards for All England** (tnlcommunityfund.org.uk) — £300–£20k. Rolling, ~12 week decision. Backs community participation, connection and wellbeing — not sport-only. No charity registration needed; needs a constituted not-for-profit + bank account with 2 signatories. You can hold only one at a time. Usually the biggest realistic single cheque. The Case for Support is written to serve this.
- **Tesco Stronger Starts** (tescostrongerstarts.org.uk) — up to ~£1,500 (1st/2nd/3rd by customer vote). Equipment/running costs, no match funding. Rolling, quarterly in-store vote.
- **Co-op Local Community Fund** (causes.coop.co.uk) — member-fundraising model, community projects. Check current round dates.
- **Local authority / council community & ward funds** — postcode-gated. Identify the club's actual borough/council first. Often Low effort, community/wellbeing focus.
- **Sported** (sported.org.uk) — support and some funding routes for community sport groups; some strands are demographic-specific (check).
- **Asda Foundation**, **Greggs Foundation**, **Aviva Community/Communities Fund** — vary by year and theme; verify current focus. (Greggs has historically listed sports clubs as unlikely to be funded; Aviva has run financial-wellbeing/climate-only themes — check before spending effort.)

## Honest notes
- **Disease/health charities (e.g. sepsis, cancer charities)** generally do NOT grant money to sports clubs — they receive donations and fund research. A club can fundraise *for* them and cite the relationship, but should not expect a grant *from* them. Health/wellbeing *community* funds (Lottery, council, Sport England) are the route for a cause-driven club.
- Gender/age-restricted funds are common in football. Always match against the verified club make-up from Step 1.
- "Where is the club" matters twice: the county FA (football funds) and the local authority (council funds). Confirm both.
