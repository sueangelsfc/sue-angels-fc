**ROLLING — apply at least 12 weeks before you need funds**

- **Amount:** £300 to £15,000
- **What it covers:** Getting more people physically active, reducing inactivity, tackling inequalities. Covers activity costs, basic equipment, running costs.

- **Key eligibility requirements:** Must have at least three non-related trustees or directors who do not live together, with equally distributed voting rights. ## SECTION 3 — CASE FOR SUPPORT
*(Reusable text — adapt the opening sentence to the specific funder's language)*

---

Sue's Angels FC is a grassroots women's football club based in Hanworth, South West London, founded in 2025 in memory of Susan Anne Martin, who was taken by sepsis. From its first day, the club has carried two purposes: to give women in the local community a place to play competitive, organised football, and to keep Susan's name alive by raising awareness of sepsis, a condition that kills around 48,000 people in the UK every year and remains widely misunderstood by the public.

In our inaugural 2025/26 season, playing in League Ten of the Southern Sunday Football League, Sue's Angels FC won every single match. Eighteen played, eighteen won, unbeaten. The club has been promoted to League Eight for 2026/27, an achievement no other team in the league's recent history has matched in a debut campaign. That record is not just a sporting result; it is proof that a community can build something meaningful from grief, and do it with excellence. The players, coaches and volunteers who make this club run have given up Sunday mornings, weekday evenings and hours of personal time to create something that stands for more than results on a pitch.

We are now entering our second season with a growing squad, a committed coaching staff and a clear set of goals: to deepen our roots in Hanworth, to welcome more women into the game who have never played before, and to use matchdays and club events to share information about sepsis recognition and prevention. Our players bring the message into their own networks, their workplaces and their families. We also carry Susan's memory with dignity and warmth, believing, as we say as a club, that what we do in life echoes in eternity.

The funding we are seeking will allow us to [insert specific project here — e.g. purchase match-quality kit and equipment / fund a full season of pitch hire and referee costs / run a sepsis awareness matchday event series / support a Level 1 coaching qualification for a new female coach]. Every pound spent here goes directly into giving women in South West London a safe, welcoming and competitive space to play football, and into honouring a woman whose life deserved to be remembered.

## SECTION 4 — READINESS CHECKLIST

Go through this before submitting any application. Items marked [GATHER] need the club to obtain or prepare them. Items marked [CONFIRM] need a quick check.

### Legal and Governance
- [ ] **Club constitution / rules document** [GATHER] — A written constitution is required by the National Lottery, Sport England, Football Foundation and virtually every other funder. If the club does not yet have one, The FA provides a template constitution for grassroots clubs. Contact your county FA for a copy.
- [ ] **Named committee with defined roles** [CONFIRM] — Most funders require at minimum a Chair, Secretary and Treasurer who are three separate people not living together. Confirm who holds each role.
- [ ] **Equality and inclusion policy** [GATHER] — A one-page written policy is required by most funders. Your county FA will have a template.
- [ ] **Safeguarding policy** [GATHER] — Required by all football funders and most general community funders. The FA has a mandatory safeguarding policy template. If any players are under 18, a named Club Welfare Officer with valid DBS is legally required.
- [ ] **DBS checks** [CONFIRM] — Are all coaches and relevant volunteers DBS checked? Required by Football Foundation and most community funders, especially if under-18s are involved.

### Financial
- [ ] **Bank account in the club's name** [GATHER] — Must be a dedicated club account, not a personal account. Must require at least two signatories to authorise any withdrawal. This is a hard requirement for the National Lottery, Sport England and most others. If not yet in place, open one as a priority.
- [ ] **Bank statements (last 3 months)** [CONFIRM] — Several funders (Co-op, Barclays/Sported) require a recent bank statement dated within the last 3 months, showing the club name, sort code and account number.
- [ ] **Annual accounts or income/expenditure summary** [GATHER] — Some funders (Greggs, others) require at least one year of filed accounts. As a club founded in 2025, you may need a simple income/expenditure statement covering the 25/26 season. Ask your Treasurer to prepare this.
- [ ] **Project budget with line items** [GATHER] — Every grant application needs a clear budget showing exactly what you will spend the money on and how you know the costs (obtain quotes where possible). Two supplier quotes are required for Football Foundation equipment grants.

### Football-specific
- [ ] **County FA affiliation number** [CONFIRM] — This is the single most important piece of administrative information for football-specific grants. Confirm whether the club is affiliated via London FA or Surrey FA, and obtain the affiliation reference number from your league secretary or county FA.
- [ ] **Football Foundation portal registration** [GATHER] — Create a free account at the Football Foundation website before applying for any of their grants. Registration requires the affiliation number.
- [ ] **England Football / Whole Game System (WGS) registration** [CONFIRM] — The club should have a WGS record via The FA. Check with your county FA that the club is correctly registered for the 26/27 season.
- [ ] **League affiliation and entry confirmation for 26/27** [CONFIRM] — Confirm in writing that the club is entered into League Eight of the Southern Sunday Football League for 26/27. Some funders ask for proof of ongoing organised activity.

### Story and Evidence
- [ ] **Club logo / crest** [CONFIRM] — Required for grant applications and any marketing material submitted with them.
- [ ] **Brief summary of the club's story** [CONFIRM] — The Case for Support in Section 3 of this document covers this. Adapt it per application.
- [ ] **Evidence of need / community impact** [GATHER] — Gather any data available: number of players registered, number of women who had not played football before joining, sessions held per week, any community events run. Even basic numbers strengthen an application significantly.
- [ ] **Connection to sepsis awareness activity** [CONFIRM] — Be specific about what the club has done or plans to do. Examples: a dedicated sepsis awareness matchday, a social media campaign, distributing UK Sepsis Trust leaflets, fundraising for Sepsis Research FEAT. Funders respond better to concrete plans than broad intentions.
