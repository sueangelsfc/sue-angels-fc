Our cause

Founded in her name. Playing for Sue.

Sue's Angels was started in memory of Susan Anne Martin. We play so her name stays part of something good — and we use every matchday to help people spot sepsis early, when it matters most.

Sepsis can look like flu or a chest infection at first. If someone is deteriorating fast, trust your instinct and ask the question: “Could it be sepsis?”
