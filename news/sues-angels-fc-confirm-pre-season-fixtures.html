Sue’s Angels FC Confirm Pre-Season Fixtures

20 July 2026 · 1 min read · Sue's Angels FC

Sue’s Angels FC can confirm our pre-season fixture schedule as preparations begin for the 2026/27 season.

After a memorable campaign that saw the club continue its rise, attention now turns to the hard work ahead. The coming weeks will provide the squad with valuable opportunities to build fitness, develop match sharpness, and continue strengthening the foundations that have been established over the past year.

The management team has arranged six fixtures throughout August, with a mixture of home and away matches against a range of opposition.

Confirmed Fixtures

2nd August
Pure Football (A)

9th August
Galacticos (A)

12th August
Kingsmeadow (H)

16th August
Brentford Town (A)

23rd August
Kew Antigua (A)

30th August
BPR (H)

Looking Ahead

Pre-season is about far more than results. It is an opportunity for players to return to the pitch, for new faces to settle into the group, and for the squad to continue developing ahead of another important season.

Every fixture will provide valuable minutes and help the management team assess preparations as the countdown to the new campaign continues.

We look forward to seeing supporters at the games and thank everyone for their continued backing as we begin the next chapter together.

The journey continues.
