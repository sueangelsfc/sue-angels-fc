Interview

Sama on two goals from right wing-back

1 November 2026 · 2 min read · Sue's Angels FC

Interview1:41 Sama on two goals from right wing-back 1 Nov 2026Sue’s Angels FC

Samakab Nur came on at right wing-back in the 70th minute at The Reeves, scored twice in four minutes and took Man of the Match in the 6–1 win over TSM Rovers. He talked us through both finishes afterwards, and which one he is claiming.

Right wing-back is not my position at all normally, but I just thought, you know what, let me give it a go.Hear him say it 0:51

0:15You came on at right wing-back and ended up scoring two. What was going through your mind when you entered the game, and did you ever imagine having that kind of impact?

I’ve always got confidence in myself making an impact in this team. So as soon as I came on, I just wanted to try and get myself in a good position and hopefully I’ll get the ball, and luckily I did, for two. Especially Jon’s ball and Frazier’s ball. Appreciate it, with the assists.

0:41Two goals from right wing-back. Talk us through both finishes, and which one are you claiming as your favourite?

No, right wing-back is not my position at all normally, but I just thought, you know what, let me give it a go. The way the team is set up, I can attack more than defend, because we’ve got a really strong back three. So it gave me the confidence to go up a bit more.

I’d say my favourite was my second one, with Frazier. He saw me, he looked up, he crossed it in first time. The ball was too good for me to even bother taking another touch. Just touch it in the bottom corner. And yeah man, I appreciate the team cheering me on. It was a nice little touch.

The match

Two goals and almost a third after coming on at right wing-back was some contribution, and it made it five wins from five. The full match report has all six goals in order.

The captain and the manager spoke after the game as well: Dan on the width that opened TSM up, and the gaffer on the response to Juizo.
