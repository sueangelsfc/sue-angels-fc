Match Report Brief: Sue's Angels FC 2-0 Hillside Elite FC Blues

The story ended exactly how everyone connected with Sue's Angels FC would have hoped.

On the final day of the Southern Sunday Division Ten season, Sue's Angels FC defeated Hillside Elite FC Blues 2-0 at The Reeves Sports Club to complete a historic perfect league campaign.

The victory means Sue's Angels finish the season with 18 wins from 18 league matches, becoming the first club since the league's inception in 2021/22 to complete a Division Ten season with a 100% record.

The match carried huge significance for all three clubs involved.

Hillside arrived knowing that victory would secure promotion, while Brockwell Violets had already completed their season and were relying on Sue's Angels to take points from Hillside in order to secure second place and promotion.

With promotion, records and history all on the line, the atmosphere reflected the importance of the occasion.

Match Summary:

  • Sue's Angels started professionally and controlled large periods of the game
  • Daniel McLane opened the scoring in the 20th minute following a corner kick
  • Daniel continued his outstanding goalscoring record from set pieces, having become one of the club's most dangerous threats from corners throughout the season
  • Hillside remained competitive and organised, knowing promotion depended on the result
  • The referee managed the occasion well throughout despite the pressure and significance surrounding the fixture

Second Half:

  • Fraizer-Isaïas Osunkoya doubled the lead in the 48th minute
  • The goal came after a superb solo run from Fraizer
  • Alfie provided the assist before Fraizer drove forward and finished to make it 2-0
  • The goal effectively settled the contest and gave Sue's Angels control of the match

Disciplinary Moment:

  • A Hillside player was sent off during the second half after receiving a second yellow card
  • The dismissal made Hillside's task even more difficult as they chased the result required for promotion

Key Outcomes:

  • Sue's Angels complete a perfect 18 wins from 18 league season
  • Sue's Angels finish on 54 points, extending their Division Ten points record
  • Brockwell Violets secure promotion alongside Sue's Angels
  • Hillside Elite FC Blues miss out on promotion
  • Sue's Angels finish with the best defensive record in Division Ten history, conceding just 11 league goals
  • Sue's Angels finish with 90 league goals scored
  • Sue's Angels remain the highest-scoring team across the Southern Sunday Football League pyramid

Season Context:

The result caps an extraordinary debut season for Sue's Angels FC.

Achievements include:

  • Division Ten Champions
  • Promotion secured with four games remaining
  • Cup Final appearance
  • Quarter Final appearance
  • Additional strong cup run
  • 33 matches played across all competitions
  • 137 goals scored across all competitions
  • 25 goals conceded across all competitions
  • 88% overall win percentage
