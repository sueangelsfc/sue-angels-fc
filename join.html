Get involved · 26/27 season

Pull on the shirt.

New season, new league, same ambition. After going unbeaten to win League Ten, we step up to League Eight for 26/27, and we're building the squad and the family that takes us there. Players, volunteers and supporters all welcome.

Get in touch → See who we need

Squad 26/27

Who we're looking for.

Outfield

Players across the pitch

Defenders, midfielders and forwards for open-age Sunday football. Any level, we care more about commitment and attitude than a CV.

Between the sticks

A goalkeeper

We're after a reliable keeper to own the box and organise the back line for the League Eight campaign.

Off the pitch

Volunteers & committee

Matchday help, media, physio, coaching and fundraising. If you want to be part of it without the boots, there's a place for you.

What to expect

More than a
team.

We're a football family from south-west London, founded in memory of Susan Anne Martin and playing for sepsis awareness. Champions on the pitch, and there for each other off it.

  • 01
    Sunday-league footballLeague Eight matches plus pre-season friendlies through the autumn.
  • 02
    A proper set-upHome at The Reeves in Hanworth, a full kit, and a squad that trains and travels together.
  • 03
    Playing for a causeEvery game raises awareness of sepsis and keeps Sue's name alive.
  • 04
    Room to growFrom your first training session to a league medal, last season proved what this group can do.

How it works

Three steps in.

  1. 1

    Say hello

    Message us with your position and a bit about yourself. No trial video needed.

  2. 2

    Come down

    Join a training session or pre-season friendly, meet the lads, get a feel for it.

  3. 3

    Sign on

    Get registered, collect your kit, and you're an Angel for the 26/27 season.

Ready when you are

Become an Angel.

Drop us a line and we'll get you to the next session. Trials, volunteering, and sponsorship all open for 26/27.
