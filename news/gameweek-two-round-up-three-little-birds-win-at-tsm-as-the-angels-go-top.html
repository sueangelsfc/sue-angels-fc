Gameweek Two round-up: Three Little Birds win at TSM as the Angels go top

15 September 2026 · 6 min read · Sue's Angels FC

News Gameweek Two round-up: Three Little Birds win at TSM as the Angels go top 15 Sept 2026Sue’s Angels FC

Gameweek Two was the first full weekend of League Eight. Eight of the division’s nine clubs played on Sunday 13 September, and for five of them it was their first match of the season.

Only two matches were played in Gameweek One. Sue’s Angels beat Three Little Birds 2–1 at The Reeves Sports Club, and TSM Rovers won 4–1 away at Junction Elite, which put TSM top of the early table on goal difference.

At Richardson Evans Memorial Playing Fields, Makheyi Chilumbi scored a hat-trick for TSM and Vincent Narayna scored the other. Liam O'mara leonard’s goal for Junction Elite came in the 18th minute.

Junction Elite were the one club without a match in Gameweek Two.

The results

Three Little Birds win at the early leaders

A week after losing 2–1 at The Reeves, Three Little Birds went to TSM Rovers and won 4–3. It was the only away win of the weekend, and it took TSM’s record from played one, won one to played two, won one.

Both clubs told the story of the match on Instagram. Three Little Birds led 2–0 at half-time and scored twice more at the start of the second half to go 4–0 up. TSM then had a man sent off after 50 minutes.

Down to ten men, TSM scored three times and finished the match pushing for an equaliser. Three Little Birds called it a nervy finish; TSM said they almost completed the comeback but ran out of time.

Three Little Birds’ graphic names Victor as the scorer of a hat-trick and their Man of the Match, with TJ also among their scorers. TSM wore their new green home kit for the first time.

Both clubs sent their best wishes to a Three Little Birds midfielder who was injured during the match.

Both clubs now have three points from two matches.

TSM’s goals have come from two players. Between them, Makheyi Chilumbi and Vincent Narayna have scored all seven of TSM’s League Eight goals, and Chilumbi’s four put him level with Charlie Dunkley at the top of the division’s scorers.

Full-Time’s opening-day line-ups show three of Chilumbi’s goals came against Junction Elite and one of Narayna’s, so against Three Little Birds Narayna scored twice and Chilumbi once.

Winning starts for Brockwell and Bristol City

Brockwell Violets, familiar from League Ten last season, opened their League Eight campaign with a 2–0 home win over Pure Football FC 1st Team, and a clean sheet.

Bristol City (London) Supporters beat Barnes Stormers 2–1 at home, with goals from Jack Lowe and James Hayes.

Both have played one match, so each has a game in hand on the clubs around them.

The Angels go top

Sue’s Angels beat Haydons Park 8–1 at The Reeves. Ade Owolana and Charlie Dunkley both scored hat-tricks, Leon Burnett scored a penalty and Elis Brumpton headed in from a corner. Haydons Park’s goal was a penalty by their captain, Aaron McNamara.

The full match report has every goal.

The result makes Sue’s Angels the only club in League Eight with two wins. They have six points and a goal difference of plus eight, three points clear of the four clubs on three.
