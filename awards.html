Recognition · 25/26

Awards & honours.

A title is won by a squad, but it is decided in moments by individuals. These are the players the club picked out across 25/26, month by month, match by match, and on the night it was all counted up.

01The record of the season League Ten · 25/26

The best defensive record in League Ten history.

11goals conceded in 18 league games

No side has gone through a League Ten season conceding fewer. Thirteen fewer than the next best defence in the division, and less than a goal a game across the whole campaign.

  • 0.73Conceded a game
  • 7League clean sheets
  • 4Longest shut-out run

Luke Munns took the Defensive Record Award for it, though the number belongs to a back line that defended as one all season.

Player of the Month

September · 25/26

Malachi Mullings

Attacking midfielder

A huge congratulations to Malachi on being named Sue’s Angels FC Player of the Month for September.

Malachi has played a key role in our strong start to the season, consistently making an impact in the final third. With 1 goal and 4 assists in just 3 games, his contributions have been vital to the team’s early success.

A well-deserved award and a fantastic way to start the campaign.

Congratulations, Malachi!

Centre back

Congratulations to Dan on being named Sue’s Angels FC Player of the Month for February.

Dan’s commitment, consistency and performances throughout the month made him a standout contributor for the team. His hard work and dedication have not gone unnoticed, and this award is thoroughly deserved.

Congratulations, Dan, and thank you for your efforts throughout February.

Player of the Month

March · 25/26

Daniel McLane

Centre back

Congratulations to Dan on being named Sue’s Angels FC Player of the Month for March, making it two awards in a row after also winning in February.

Dan continued his outstanding season with a series of influential performances from centre-back. Despite playing in defence, he contributed an impressive 4 goals, helped the team keep 3 clean sheets, and picked up a Man of the Match award along the way.

His leadership, consistency and quality at both ends of the pitch were key as Sue’s Angels FC went through March unbeaten, making this latest accolade thoroughly deserved.

Congratulations, Dan!

0425/26 awards night 7 winners

End of season awards.

  • Top Assister

    Charlie Dunkley

    Striker

    Relentless all season. Energy, intelligence and selfless running made him a constant threat, and his assists and link play drove our attacking success.

  • Defensive Record Award

    Luke Munns

    Goalkeeper

    The goalkeeper every championship side needs. Calm, commanding and full of match-winning saves as the Angels set a historic defensive record.

  • Goal of the Season

    Malachi Mullings

    Attacking midfielder

    A stunning strike against Mala Vida, pure quality. A huge part of the season before injury cut it short. We can’t wait to see him back in yellow.

  • Top Goalscorer

    Frazier-Isaías Osunkoya

    Right attacking midfielder

    Goals, goals and more goals. Movement, composure and a finish in him at any moment. When the team needed one, Fraizer usually delivered.

  • Clubman of the Year

    Jim El Bayati

    Right centre back

    A leader, role model and true club servant. As he closes his playing career he leaves the culture and standards that drive the club. A true legend.

  • Players’ Player of the Year

    Daniel McLane

    Centre back

    Voted for by his teammates. Solid as a rock in defence and a real weapon from set pieces. A warrior who set the standard every single week.

  • Manager’s Player of the Year

    Dean Knight

    Right wing back

    Consistency, versatility and constant improvement. Right-back, wing-back, even emergency keeper with two clean sheets. Whatever the team needed, Dean delivered.
