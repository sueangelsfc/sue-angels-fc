# Case for Support template

A reusable narrative the club pastes into applications, adapting the opening line to each funder's language. British spelling, warm but factual, no em or en dashes. Aim for 3-4 short paragraphs.

## Structure
1. **Who we are + why we exist** — name, make-up, location, founded when, and the club's purpose. If there is a cause or memorial, lead with it; funders fund missions, not just sport.
2. **Proof we deliver** — concrete record: league, results, squad size, sessions run, volunteers. Even small numbers show capacity and reduce the funder's risk.
3. **Who benefits and how** — the community impact in plain terms: participation, wellbeing/mental health, connection, the cause being advanced. Tie this to the funder's stated priorities.
4. **What the money enables** — one paragraph with a clear ask, leaving a [bracket] to insert the specific project (kit, pitch hire, referee costs, an awareness event, a coaching qualification).

## Filled example (adapt every bracket; verify every fact first)
> [Club name] is a grassroots [make-up, e.g. men's Sunday-league] football club based in [town, local authority], founded in [year] [optional: in memory of (name) / to (cause)]. From the start the club has carried two purposes: to give [who] in the local community a place to play organised, competitive football and to support their wellbeing through the game, and to [the cause or community aim].
>
> In our [season] we [proof of delivery: e.g. competed in (league), played (N) matches, grew to a squad of (N)]. That record reflects a committed group of players, coaches and volunteers who give their own time to build something that means more than results on a pitch.
>
> We are now entering [next phase] with clear goals: to deepen our roots in [place], to welcome more [who] into the game, and to [community/cause activity]. Our players carry this into their own families, workplaces and networks.
>
> The funding we are seeking will allow us to [insert specific project here, e.g. purchase match kit and equipment / fund a season of pitch hire and referee costs / run a community awareness event series / support a coaching qualification]. Every pound goes directly into giving [who] in [place] a safe, welcoming and competitive space to play, and into [the cause].

## Guidance
- Match the opening sentence to the funder: a sport funder wants "football"; a community funder wants "community wellbeing and connection".
- Quantify wherever honest numbers exist. Vague intentions read weaker than concrete plans.
- Keep one clear, costed ask per application. Funders reject sprawling wish-lists.
