03The report Written by the club

The full report.

Angels hit six to make it five wins from five

Back at The Reeves and back among the goals. We returned to league action for the first time since September with a 6–1 win over TSM Rovers, making it five wins from five and moving three points clear at the top.

LJ, Charlie and Ade put us 3–1 ahead at the break before the substitutes got involved. Frazier added our fourth, while Sama came on at right wing-back to score twice and take home Man of the Match.

After the frustration of going out on penalties against Juizo last weekend, it was a good way to respond. We now have 15 points from a possible 15, with 26 goals scored and just four conceded.

Finding a way through a compact five

TSM started with a clear plan, setting up in a compact 4–5–1 and looking to keep things tight. With plenty of bodies behind the ball, the challenge was to move them around and find a way through.

We started brightly and thought Charlie had done exactly that after five minutes, only for the offside flag to go up. It looked a close call, with Charlie appearing to be behind the centre-back when the ball was played, but we didn’t have to wait too much longer for the opener.

We got an early goal which was chalked off, but we looked like a different team to last week in front of goal, which is what I wanted this week.

The opener came in the 16th minute after TSM struggled to clear a corner. Dean’s delivery reached Dan at the back post, but his effort was blocked by a defender. Andy picked up the loose ball near the touchline and recycled it, finding Stewart, who brought it down on his chest and attempted a volley. He didn’t catch it cleanly, but the ball fell to LJ, who swivelled and struck a lovely right-footed volley to put us 1–0 up.

Charlie got his goal eight minutes later. Jimi sent him through on the right side of the box, and he lifted a lovely lob over the goalkeeper to double our lead.

Their goalkeeper keeps them in it

There was some lovely combination play down our left, where Mike, Andy and Ade linked up to outnumber TSM. One move ended with Ade delivering a well-weighted cross for Adam, whose header went straight at the goalkeeper. Either side of him and it could have been 3–0.

Further chances followed, but the TSM goalkeeper pulled off a string of excellent saves to keep it at two. Those stops kept the visitors in the game, and in the 33rd minute they pulled one back.

There wasn’t much anyone could do about their goal. A superb free-kick flew straight into the top-right corner, leaving Luke no chance. A proper postage-stamp finish, and one that gave TSM fresh belief.

Ade restores the cushion

We continued to threaten, particularly from set pieces. Ade and Dean’s corners from either side were causing TSM problems, with the visitors struggling to deal with the deliveries into their box.

That pressure paid off in the 41st minute. Following a corner, LJ found Ade inside the box, and he guided a cultured left-footed volley home to make it 3–1 and restore our two-goal lead.

It was an important goal at a good time. TSM had given themselves a way back into the match, but we went into half-time with a little more breathing space and three quality finishes to show for our work.

In the first half we were quite patient with it, we were keeping it at the back and weren’t forcing it, and then when the gaps appeared we put it through.

The bench makes its mark

Mike spent much of the afternoon trying to win his own battle with their goalkeeper. He kept getting into positions to score, but the keeper kept finding an answer, producing several fine saves to keep him off the scoresheet.

On the hour, Stephen brought on Bas, Frazier and Jon for Jimi, Charlie and LJ. Sama then replaced Dean at right wing-back in the 70th minute, and the bench soon made its mark.

Right wing-back is not my position at all normally, but I just thought, you know what, let me give it a go.Hear him say it 0:51

Frazier got our fourth in the 73rd minute. Stewart played the ball through, and a moment of hesitation between the centre-back and goalkeeper gave him his chance. With both waiting for the other to react, Frazier nipped in, rounded the keeper and slotted home with his left foot, leaving the defender on the line with no chance.

Jon then helped make it five with some brilliant work down the right. He had been giving their left-back a difficult time since coming on, threatening to beat him inside and out. This time he went down the outside, reached the byline and drove into the box before putting the ball on a plate for Sama to finish from close range in the 77th minute.

Luckily I did, for two. Especially Jon’s ball and Frazier’s ball.Hear him say it 0:35

Four minutes later, Sama was there again.

Some lovely, controlled football between Mike, Andy and Frazier opened things up down the left. Frazier delivered the cross, and Sama arrived to finish with his right foot and make it 6–1.

The ball was too good for me to even bother taking another touch.Hear him say it 1:15

He nearly capped his afternoon with a hat-trick, too, but the TSM goalkeeper produced another outstanding save to deny him a third. Even with the game beyond the visitors, their keeper kept making us work and deserves real credit for his performance.

Two goals and almost a third after coming on at right wing-back was some contribution. Sama’s runs into the box paid off, earning him Man of the Match and rounding off a good afternoon for the substitutes.

Liam also joined the action late on as we saw the game out and secured another three points.

The win makes it 23 straight league victories stretching back to last season’s unbeaten title win. There’s plenty of football still to play, but five wins from five is exactly the start we wanted in League Eight.

What Sama said

0:15You came on at right wing-back and ended up scoring two. What was going through your mind when you entered the game, and did you ever imagine having that kind of impact?

I’ve always got confidence in myself making an impact in this team. So as soon as I came on, I just wanted to try and get myself in a good position and hopefully I’ll get the ball, and luckily I did, for two. Especially Jon’s ball and Frazier’s ball. Appreciate it, with the assists.

0:41Two goals from right wing-back. Talk us through both finishes, and which one are you claiming as your favourite?

No, right wing-back is not my position at all normally, but I just thought, you know what, let me give it a go. The way the team is set up, I can attack more than defend, because we’ve got a really strong back three. So it gave me the confidence to go up a bit more.

I’d say my favourite was my second one, with Frazier. Frazier saw me, he looked up, he crossed it in first time. The ball was too good for me to even bother taking another touch. Just touch it in the bottom corner. And yeah man, I appreciate the team cheering me on. It was a nice little touch.

What the captain said

Six goals and another convincing win. What impressed you most about the way we played?

The way we played today, we actually got the ball down a lot more. TSM sort of allowed us to do that, where some teams come onto us and shut us down, but we got the ball, knocked it around, and especially in the second half we were getting it wide and either dribbling it or whipping balls in. It was really effective. I think two goals in succession came from balls coming in.

Was there a moment or a passage of play that summed up the performance?

I think in the first half, where we were quite patient with it, we were keeping it at the back and weren’t forcing it, and then when the gaps appeared we put it through. It wasn’t a particular passage of play. I’d probably say the nicest one involved, I think it was Liam, then it went down to Frazier, and Frazier just whipped the ball in at pace and Sama got on the end of it. That was my goal of the game.

But really, when we’re being patient and then getting it into those wide spots, you’ve got Dean running on, Andy and Mike down the left, and using that width is where we excel.

We’ve had a fantastic start to life in League Eight and there’s clearly a lot of belief in the group. What are you enjoying most about this squad at the moment?

They’re a really good bunch of lads, there’s not a bad egg in there. People will get frustrated when they’re not in the starting line-up, but all in all they’re good guys, they’re a good bunch of lads, and that’s why I enjoy it. I used to play in competitive teams years ago and it can be quite prickly in some changing rooms. This changing room is good lads, so that’s why I enjoy it.

What the manager said

After the disappointment of going out on penalties last week, we responded today with six goals and another three points. How pleased are you with our reaction?

I’m very pleased. We started the first half very quickly. We got an early goal which was chalked off, but we looked like a different team to last week in front of goal, which is what I wanted this week.

We struggled to find a breakthrough against Juizo last week, but today we scored six. What do you feel we did differently in the final third?

We’re more composed. We look more alive in the final third. We look hungry. We had more desire to score a goal this week, and that’s proved with the six goals, and I’ll be honest, I think it could have been a lot more.

Sama came off the bench at right wing-back, scored two and picked up Man of the Match. How impressed were you with his contribution, and what does it say about the options in the squad?

Sama is very well liked in the team, and the celebration after and during when he scored the goals was a pleasure to see. Everyone was really happy, and that goes for the whole squad. I think Dan mentioned it about the group that we have: a very good group, and there’s no bad egg, he said, in this group. That’s only positive for the team.

How pleased are you with the confidence and the togetherness the group is showing?

The camaraderie and the spirit in the players after the game, I thought that was the best part, that they’ve come off the pitch and everyone being together. It felt like last season when it was a feel-good: we’ve come off the pitch and no one’s heads down or annoyed, everyone seemed in a good mood, which was a pleasure to see.

We travel to Brockwell Violets next Sunday, a side we know well from last season and who, before this weekend, were also unbeaten. How important will it be to carry today’s result into next week?

The momentum is going to be what we need, especially when you’ve performed and scored goals. It will only give us confidence for next week and we’re looking forward to it. It’s another big game, it’s a big game for them, it’s a big game for us, and we’re the top two, so the confidence we’ve gained from this week will help us for next week.

Next up, first meets second

We head to Clapham Common on Sunday 8 November to face Brockwell Violets, with kick-off at 10:30am. They sit three points behind us after their 1–0 defeat to Three Little Birds, giving next weekend’s meeting a little extra edge.

Last season we finished champions and Brockwell runners-up. Now we meet again in League Eight with both clubs at the top. A win would put us six points clear of them; a win for Brockwell would bring them level on points.

Still early days, but this is one to look forward to.

Thank you to TSM Rovers for the game. We wish them all the best for the rest of the season.
