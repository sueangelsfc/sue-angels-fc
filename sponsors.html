02Why partner 18 from 18

More than a logo on a shirt.

Sue’s Angels is not a typical grassroots side. We are League Ten champions, unbeaten in our first season, built around a cause that matters, with a growing audience that puts local businesses in front of the right people.

18from 18 The league record your brand goes on.

  1. Champions, with momentum

    Played 18, won 18, promoted to League Eight. Your brand backs a winning, rising club, not a hopeful start-up.

  2. A club with a cause

    Founded in memory of Susan Anne Martin, and supporting sepsis awareness. Backing us means standing behind something that matters.

  3. Local roots, real reach

    Home at The Reeves in Hanworth, serving Kingston, Sunbury, Staines and south-west London, and growing across Instagram, TikTok and the web.

  4. A package, not a slot

    No fixed tiers. We build the partnership around your business, from kit and content to matchday and more.
