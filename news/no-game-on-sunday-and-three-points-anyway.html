No game on Sunday, and three points anyway

20 November 2026 · 3 min read · Sue's Angels FC

News No game on Sunday, and three points anyway 20 Nov 2026Sue’s Angels FC

There is no football at The Reeves this Sunday. Brockwell Violets could not raise a side, and on Friday the league awarded us the match: three points without anybody kicking a ball.

It is the second time this season that a League Eight opponent has not made it to The Reeves, and the first time anything came of it. Mousa Mountain folded in October, two days before they were due here, and the fixture went with the club: no match, no points, and a row quietly missing from the table. Brockwell are still in the league and still going. These three count.

We played them a fortnight ago and won 6-1 at Clapham Common, with ten men for most of the second half. That makes seven league games this season and seven wins.

The table, with the walkover counted

Full-Time has it in. Seven played, seven won, twenty-one points, and the goal columns exactly where Clapham Common left them: a walkover carries no goals in this league, so we take the three points and nothing else moves.

Brockwell drop to third on goal difference without playing. Three Little Birds have twelve from five and two games in hand on everybody near them, which is worth remembering before anyone calls six points a comfortable lead.

The one row nobody is sure about

Pure Football FC 1st Team are still in that table, on one point from three games. They have not played since 4 October and were reported to have folded on 25 October. The league struck their results off once, put them back on 1 November, and they are still there today.

If they come out again, three results go with them. Ours is untouched, because we never played them: Brockwell lose a 2-0, TSM Rovers a 4-0 and Bristol City a 3-3.
