The gaffer on a statement, and ten men seeing it out

8 November 2026 · 3 min read · Sue's Angels FC

Interview The gaffer on a statement, and ten men seeing it out 8 Nov 2026Sue’s Angels FC

Six from six in League Eight, and the second-placed side beaten 6-1 at home with ten men for most of the second half. Stephen Epathite spoke after the win over Brockwell Violets.

It was a statement. We’re top.

How did you find the game?

I thought in the first half we were relentless in terms of the chances that we created and the possession with the ball in their half. We were on the front foot from minute one.

What impressed you about the first half?

Just the energy and the effort and the attitude and the desire and the relentlessness. It was relentless. It was that wave of being on the ball, going forward, getting into the box, having shots on goal, and that’s reflected with 5-0 at half time.

It was a bit of a game of two halves for obvious reasons. There was a questionable second yellow given to Luke. How do you think the boys reacted?

I think we’ve got a lot of older heads, a lot of sensible guys in here that know how to manage matches. When Luke got a second yellow, which I don’t believe was a second yellow, they managed the game really well. They managed to score another goal. The game management was brilliant. A lot of older heads, a lot of smart footballers, a lot of smart men that know how to react in those situations.

It was first against second. What message does that send out?

It was a statement. We’re top. We’ve won 6-1. I think that if we had finished with eleven it could have been double, and I think we were in that mood. It sends out a message that we are still strong. We are going for this league title and we should be confident. We are confident at the moment, and that shows on the pitch.

Next up is The Station at home in the cup. What are your thoughts on that one?

I don’t know much about The Station. Sometimes it’s probably best not to know about other teams. But as always, I’m worried about what we do and how we react. We’re at home. We’re strong at home. I expect them to come with a challenge, as a cup game. Both teams are going to be up for it. But as always, we will play to win the game.

Man of the Match went to Andy Allen. What was the reason?

Andy was the intensity and the effort and the attitude and the hunger. The desire and the battling that he put into that. I thought he was at it from minute one. He was clever, he won every header, and I just thought he was at it today. He really set the tone for what I wanted today.
