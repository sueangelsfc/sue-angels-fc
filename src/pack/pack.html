Sue's Angels FC The club

Who we are

A club with
a reason.

Sue's Angels FC is a London Sunday-league football club, founded in 2025 in memory of Susan Anne Martin. We play out of The Reeves in Hanworth, and in our very first season we won League Ten, unbeaten, and earned promotion for 2026/27.

More than a results machine, the club is a football family. It was built to bring people together, to honour Sue's memory, and to support sepsis awareness in everything we put her name to. "What we do in life echoes in eternity" is not a slogan to us. It is the reason the club exists.

Sue's Angels FC Our cause

In her name

Playing for
something
bigger.

The club was founded in memory of Susan Anne Martin, who we lost to sepsis. Awareness is the part we can play. We keep Sue's memory and the sepsis message alive through how we show up, on matchdays and in the community.

For a local business, partnering with Sue's Angels is a chance to stand behind a genuine cause, and to be seen doing it. As the club grows, so does the reach of that message.

Supporting sepsis awareness

Sepsis is the body's extreme response to an infection, and it can become life threatening within hours. The UK Sepsis Trust estimates it claims around 48,000 lives in the UK every year, many of them preventable with early treatment. Knowing the signs saves lives, and we help spread the word.
