Sunday is off. Here is where it leaves us

17 October 2026 · 4 min read · Sue's Angels FC

News Sunday is off. Here is where it leaves us 17 Oct 2026Sue’s Angels FC

Sunday is off. Mousa Mountain FC have folded, the league told us on Saturday evening, and the League Eight match we were due to play at The Reeves on 18 October will not happen. It is never good news when a club goes under and we wish everybody there well.

So this piece is about us: where that leaves the season, the table and the next time we play.

Five from five, and nothing to play on Sunday

We have won every match we have played this season. Twenty-four scored, three conceded, two clean sheets, and the only side in League Eight with a perfect record alongside Brockwell Violets.

The run has not been one sort of win either. Three Little Birds took us to the wire on the opening day and Luke made eight saves to see it out. Haydons Park and Barnes Stormers were swept away at home. Junction Elite and Peps All Stars were won away from The Reeves without conceding. Different afternoons, same three points.

That is the form a free Sunday now interrupts.

No points, and no change

We have not been awarded any points. The league has not yet said what becomes of Mousa Mountain’s record, and it will issue updates to the table in its own time. What we have been told is that our position should not change.

Twelve points each, and seventeen goals of difference against seven. That is the margin at the top, and it is the reason the 8-1 and the 7-1 matter as much as the 2-1 did.

Every table on this site now marks Mousa Mountain with an asterisk, so nobody reads that row of noughts as a club still waiting for its first match. When the league changes the table, the table here will follow.

Our next match is a cup tie

The President’s Cup second round, at home. We earned it at Dulwich Park on 11 October, where Ade scored twice, LJ and Dan got the others and Luke kept a second clean sheet in three.

Celtic Swans FC are the opposition and we have never played them: no league match, no cup tie, no friendly, nothing in the archive with their name on it. They play in League Seven, one division above us, and they were one of four clubs given a bye past the first round, so they come into it fresh while we have already had to win one.

The tie is at The Reeves, which the league’s bracket decides rather than a toss-up, and it has not been scheduled. The date goes on this site the moment it is confirmed.

Win it and the quarter-final is tie 23, against Deportivo Los Toros, Haydons Park or Balham and Clapham Albion. We have met one of those already this season, at home, and it finished 8-1.

What happens now

Nothing on Sunday. There is no match at The Reeves and nothing to turn up for.

After that, the aim has not moved: keep the run going, stay at the top, and go further in this cup than we managed in the last one. There is further news to post, and it will go up here first.
