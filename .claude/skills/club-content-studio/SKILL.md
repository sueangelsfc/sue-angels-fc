# Club Content Studio

The voice that makes a club feel real, proud and human.

## Voice
- **Confident, warm, never corporate.** Write like a passionate club person, not a marketer.
- British spelling. **No em dashes** (commas, periods, ·). League names exactly as the club uses them (e.g. "League Ten", not "Division").
- Big uppercase display headings on the page are automatic; write natural sentence case in the body. Open with a hook, keep paragraphs tight, end with a clear next step.

## The cause, handled with care
Many clubs carry a story of loss or a charity mission. Treat it with **dignity and hope, never grief-heavy**. Honour the person, point forward, and make the reader feel part of something. When in doubt, lighter and more hopeful.

## Content types
- **Match reports**: name the scorers, assisters and MOTM; tie the result to the season story.
- **News & announcements**: postponements, signings, milestones. For anything factual (weather, dates, stats), **verify with web search** and cite real figures — accuracy builds trust.
- **Player & staff bios**: clubs they've played for, position, what they bring on and off the pitch. Warm and specific.
- **Awards write-ups**: punchy 1–2 sentence cards plus a fuller article for the big announcements.
- **Newsletter copy**: a clean branded email that links to the issue; tone consistent with the site.

## Publishing
Articles are usually admin/CMS data (the anon key can't write them) — hand the club ready-to-paste copy, or seed evergreen/announcement posts in the code defaults so they ship directly. Dispatch **news-writer** (and **match-reporter** for reports). Part of **football-website-builder**.
