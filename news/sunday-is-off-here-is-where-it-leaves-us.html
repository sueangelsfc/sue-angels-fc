Sunday is off. Here is where it leaves us

17 October 2026 · 6 min read · Sue's Angels FC

News Sunday is off. Here is where it leaves us 17 Oct 2026Sue’s Angels FC

Sunday is off. Mousa Mountain FC have folded, the league told us on Saturday evening, and the League Eight match we were due to play at The Reeves on 18 October will not happen. It is never good news when a club goes under and we wish everybody there well.

So this piece is about us: where that leaves the season, the table and the next time we play.

Five from five, and nothing to play on Sunday

We have won every match we have played this season. Twenty-four scored, three conceded, two clean sheets, and the only side in League Eight with a perfect record alongside Brockwell Violets.

The run has not been one sort of win either. Three Little Birds took us to the wire on the opening day and Luke made eight saves to see it out. Haydons Park and Barnes Stormers were swept away at home. Junction Elite and Peps All Stars were won away from The Reeves without conceding. Different afternoons, same three points.

That is the form a free Sunday now interrupts.

No points, and no change

We have not been awarded any points, and nobody else has either. The league has now confirmed that Mousa Mountain come out of League Eight altogether, so the division is nine clubs and the fixtures against them will not be replayed or awarded.

Our position does not move. The table reads the same at the top as it did before any of this:

Twelve points each at the top, and seventeen goals of difference against seven. That is the whole margin between us and Brockwell Violets, and it is the reason the 8-1 and the 7-1 matter as much as the 2-1 did.

One practical thing follows from nine clubs: there are fewer league games to play than the season started with, so there are fewer chances to make that difference up, for us and for everybody chasing.

What comes next, and what is still to be confirmed

We play again on Sunday 25 October. The league has confirmed the Marcus Lipton tie: away at Juizo FC, 10:30, on pitch 6 at Clapham Common. The President’s Cup tie is drawn and still waiting on a date, and no further League Eight fixtures have been published.

  • Marcus Lipton Cup, first round · Juizo FC, away at Clapham Common · Sunday 25 October, 10:30
  • President’s Cup, second round · Celtic Swans FC, at The Reeves · date to be confirmed
  • League Eight · no further fixtures published yet

Both are against clubs from higher up. The league runs eleven tiers and we are in the ninth of them. Celtic Swans are in League Seven, the division directly above ours. Juizo FC are in League Four, with League Five, Six and Seven between them and us.

The President’s Cup tie

We earned it at Dulwich Park on 11 October, where Ade scored twice, LJ and Dan got the others and Luke kept a second clean sheet in three.

We have never played Celtic Swans: no league match, no cup tie, no friendly, nothing in the archive with their name on it. They were one of four clubs given a bye past the first round, so they come into it fresh while we have already had to win one. The tie is at The Reeves, which the league’s bracket decides rather than a toss-up.

Win it and the quarter-final is tie 23, against Deportivo Los Toros or Balham and Clapham Albion. It was three clubs when this was written: Haydons Park, the side we beat 8-1 here in September, went out of the cup 0-6 to Balham and Clapham Albion on 18 October. We have played neither of the two left.

The Marcus Lipton tie

The other is a first-round tie away at Juizo FC, four divisions up. We have never played them either.

It is the biggest draw we are in: 106 ties in all, 44 of them in the first round. Win this one and the second round is tie 51, away to Mala Vida FC or St Matthew’s Project.

That first name will ring a bell. Mala Vida are how this cup started for us last season, beaten 2-1 away in the first round, before Argentina FC’s first team put us out 0-2 in the second. Getting past Juizo would put the same round in front of us, and possibly the same opponent.

What happens now

Nothing on 18 October. There was no match at The Reeves and nothing to turn up for.

The Sunday after is Clapham Common, and Juizo FC of League Four. The aim has not moved: keep the run going, stay at the top, and go further in these cups than we managed in the last one. As soon as the league puts a date on the President’s Cup tie, or publishes the next round of league fixtures, it will be posted here first.
