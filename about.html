05Off the field · Sue’s story UK Sepsis Trust

Why we play.

Sue’s Angels FC was built on football, friendship and togetherness, honouring the life and memory of Susan Anne Martin.

Following Sue’s passing from sepsis, the club raises awareness through charity matches and community initiatives, keeping her memory at the heart of everything.
