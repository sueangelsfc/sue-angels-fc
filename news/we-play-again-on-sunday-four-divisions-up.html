We play again on Sunday, four divisions up

19 October 2026 · 3 min read · Sue's Angels FC

News We play again on Sunday, four divisions up 19 Oct 2026Sue’s Angels FC

We have a match again. The league has confirmed our Marcus Lipton Cup first round tie: away at Juizo FC, 10:30 on Sunday 25 October, on pitch 6 at Clapham Common.

It is our first match in a fortnight, and the biggest step up we have taken all season.

Five from five, and two Sundays without a game

Nothing has gone wrong. We have simply not had a match.

The last league game was Barnes Stormers on 27 September. The round on 4 October did not involve us. We won the President’s Cup tie at Peps All Stars on 11 October, and the following Sunday was taken off us when Mousa Mountain folded.

So the record stands where it did a fortnight ago: five competitive matches, five wins, 24 scored and 3 conceded. Ade Owolana has 9 of those goals and Charlie Dunkley 7. We are top of League Eight with four wins from four.

Four divisions up

Juizo FC play in League Four. We are in League Eight. League Five, Six and Seven sit between us, and this league runs eleven tiers in all.

We have never played them. No league match, no cup tie, no friendly, nothing in the archive with their name on it, which means there is nothing here to tell you about how they play. Sunday is the first time anybody at this club sees them.

A cup draw does not care about divisions, and the only thing four of them between two clubs guarantees is that nobody is expecting much of us. That is a reasonable place to start.

A year to the week

This is the same cup that gave us our longest run last season, and almost the same weekend.

On 26 October 2025 we won 2-1 away at Mala Vida FC in the first round. On 30 November we went out 0-2 at Argentina FC’s first team in the second.

Win on Sunday and the second round is tie 51, away to Mala Vida FC or St Matthew’s Project. So getting through would put us back in the same round we reached last year, possibly against the same club we beat to get there.

It is the biggest draw we are in: 106 ties in all, and 44 clubs meet in the first round alone. Ours is tie 2.

The other one is still waiting

The President’s Cup second round against Celtic Swans FC is drawn, it is at The Reeves, and the league has not yet put a date on it. No further League Eight fixtures have been published either. Both go up here as soon as they land.

Where and when

  • Sunday 25 October, 10:30
  • Clapham Common, pitch 6
  • Supreme Trophies Marcus Lipton Cup, first round
  • Juizo FC v Sue’s Angels FC

The clocks go back that morning, so it is a genuine hour in hand rather than a lost one. Come down if you are near: it is a public common, it is free, and an away end of any size makes a difference at this level.
