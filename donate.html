Donate

Keep the Angels playing.

Sue's Angels FC is run by volunteers and paid for by the people around it. A donation goes into the football: the pitch, the referee, the kit and the fees that put a team out on a Sunday morning.

Donate securely → (opens Stripe in a new tab) Two ways to give

Secure payment by Stripe

You choose the amount. Any amount is a help.

01Where it goes The football

Where the money goes.

  • Pitch hire

    A pitch and changing rooms for every home match, booked and paid for by the club.

  • Match fees

    The fees that come with playing in the league and the county cup, every season.

  • Referees

    A referee for every match, so the football is played properly and safely.

  • Kit

    Shirts, balls, bibs and first aid, and replacing them when a season wears them out.

02Two ways to give Secure payment

Give.

Support Sue’s Angels directly, or give to the UK Sepsis Trust in memory of Susan Anne Martin. They are separate, and both are a help.

  • Support the club

    Pitch hire, match fees, referees and kit. It all goes into running Sue’s Angels.

    Donate securely → (opens Stripe in a new tab)

    Card payment handled by Stripe. You choose the amount on Stripe’s own page, and we never see your card details.

  • Give in Sue’s memory

    Sue's Angels FC was founded in memory of Susan Anne Martin, who died of sepsis. A gift to the UK Sepsis Trust supports families and funds the work that gets people diagnosed sooner.

    Donate to the Trust → (opens sepsistrust.org in a new tab)

    You will be taken to sepsistrust.org, where the donation is handled by the Trust.

03How it works Stripe

How giving works.

  1. 1

    Press Donate securely

    Stripe’s own payment page opens in a new tab.

  2. 2

    Choose the amount

    Any amount is a help. You type it on Stripe’s page and pay by card.

  3. 3

    It reaches the club

    Stripe pays it into the club’s account. Your card details stay with Stripe and never touch this website.
