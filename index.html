Why this club exists

For Susan.

Susan Anne Martin · Founded 2025

Everything about this club begins with one person. We lost Sue to sepsis, and this club exists so her name stays part of something good, week after week. We talk openly about sepsis so that fewer families go through the same thing.

“What we do in life echoes in eternity.”

Know the signs Our story
01 The debut season

Eighteen played,
eighteen won.

Sue’s Angels entered League Ten of the Southern Sunday Football League in September 2025 as a brand new club. Nine months later they finished the league season without a single defeat, scoring 90 goals and conceding 11.

Across every competition the Angels played 33 matches, winning 29 of them and keeping 14 clean sheets. The club goes up into League Eight for 26/27.
