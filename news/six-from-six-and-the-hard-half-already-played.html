Six from six, and the hard half already played

11 November 2026 · 3 min read · Sue's Angels FC

News Six from six, and the hard half already played 11 Nov 2026Sue’s Angels FC

Eighteen matches in League Ten last season, six in League Eight this one, and no league defeat in any of them. That is 24 in a row across two divisions. It is worth saying once and then putting down, because the run is not the interesting part of this season.

Six played, six won, 32 scored and 5 conceded. Brockwell Violets have played the same six games and are six points back. The table stands at 8 November; the full division is on the league page.

The fixtures have not been kind

The easy reading of 32 goals in six games is that the draw has been generous. It has not been.

Take each opponent's record and remove their match against us, so the figures are not circular. The six clubs beaten so far have taken 1.8 points a game against everybody else in the division. The two first-round opponents still to come have taken 0.6. The three clubs sitting directly below us are the three already played, and all three were beaten.

Barnes Stormers, who have lost all five, are the only side on that list not holding their own against the rest of the league.

What a third of a season can say, and what it cannot

Six matches is a third of a league season, and arithmetic on six matches does not stretch far. The scoring rate is 5.3 a game against 6.0 in League Ten last year. The conceding rate is 0.8 against 0.7. Those are close enough together that the honest answer is that we cannot yet tell whether this side is better or worse than last season's, only that it is winning.

One thing the record does support, because it rests on all 38 matches played out across the two seasons rather than on six: the club scores far more at home than away, and concedes the same amount in both. Just over five a game at The Reeves, just under four on the road. It holds separately in each season, which is what makes it worth watching rather than a quirk of a good month.

Who has done it

Ade Owolana has 12 goals in eight matches, ten of them in the league. The division's next-best scorers, Victor Awosanya of Three Little Birds and Vincent Narayna of TSM Rovers, have six each.

Charlie Dunkley has eight goals and six assists. Leon Burnett has four and five. Dean Knight has seven assists and one goal, which is a season of a different shape and the one most easily missed.

Twenty-four players have been used in the eight competitive matches, and three of those eight were clean sheets.

The cups

Out of the Supreme Trophies Marcus Lipton Cup, beaten 4-3 on penalties at Juizo after a goalless ninety minutes on 25 October. Still in the Brian Howard President's Cup, after winning 4-0 at Peps All Stars. And into the Surrey FA Sunday Lower Junior County Cup, which starts this Sunday.

Three in a row at The Reeves

The Station in the county cup on 15 November, Brockwell Violets in the league on the 22nd, and Celtic Swans in the President's Cup on the 29th. All three at home, all three at 11:00. The fixtures page has the rest.
