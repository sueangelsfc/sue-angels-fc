# Funding readiness checklist

Go through this before submitting any application. Mark each item [GATHER] (must obtain/prepare) or [CONFIRM] (quick check). Most applications stall on the same missing items, so getting these ready first saves rejected bids.

**Biggest blocker, almost always:** a dedicated club bank account in the club's name requiring two signatories. Without it, the National Lottery, Sport England and most funders cannot pay out. If it does not exist, opening it is task #1, ahead of any writing.

## Legal and governance
- [ ] **Constitution / club rules** [GATHER] — required by nearly every funder. The FA provides a grassroots template via the county FA.
- [ ] **Named committee** [CONFIRM] — usually a Chair, Secretary and Treasurer who are three different people not living together (and not related), with equal voting rights for some funders.
- [ ] **Equality / inclusion policy** [GATHER] — one page; county FA template available.
- [ ] **Safeguarding policy** [GATHER] — required by football funders and most community funders. If any members are under 18, a named Welfare Officer with valid DBS is legally required.
- [ ] **DBS checks** [CONFIRM] — for coaches/volunteers, especially where under-18s are involved.

## Financial
- [ ] **Club bank account, 2 signatories** [GATHER] — the hard gate. Dedicated club account, not personal.
- [ ] **Recent bank statements (last 3 months)** [CONFIRM] — some funders require one showing the club name, sort code and account number.
- [ ] **Accounts or income/expenditure summary** [GATHER] — a simple statement if the club is too new for filed accounts.
- [ ] **Project budget with line items + quotes** [GATHER] — show exactly what the money buys and how costs are known. Equipment grants often need two supplier quotes.

## Football-specific
- [ ] **County FA affiliation number** [CONFIRM] — the key administrative detail for football funds. Get it from the league secretary or county FA.
- [ ] **Football Foundation portal account** [GATHER] — free; needs the affiliation number.
- [ ] **Whole Game System (WGS) registration** [CONFIRM] — club correctly registered with The FA for the current season.
- [ ] **League entry confirmation** [CONFIRM] — proof of ongoing organised activity for the upcoming season.

## Story and evidence
- [ ] **Club logo / crest** [CONFIRM] — needed for applications and any attached material.
- [ ] **Case for Support** [CONFIRM] — drafted and adapted per funder.
- [ ] **Evidence of need / impact** [GATHER] — players registered, newcomers to the sport, sessions per week, community events. Even basic numbers strengthen a bid.
- [ ] **Cause / community activity** [CONFIRM] — be specific about what the club has done or will do. Concrete plans beat broad intentions.
