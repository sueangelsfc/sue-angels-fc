Invincible Season. Outstanding Individuals. Meet Sue's Angels FC's 2025/26 Award Winners.

21 June 2026 · 4 min read · Sue's Angels FC

Following a historic and unforgettable 2025/26 campaign, Sue's Angels FC are proud to announce our End of Season Award winners.

An undefeated league season. Division Ten Champions. A record-breaking defensive campaign. History made.

While football is always a team game, these awards recognise the individuals who made an exceptional contribution to one of the greatest seasons in the club's history.

Top Assister

Charlie Dunkley

Charlie was simply relentless throughout the season.

Leading the line with energy, intelligence and determination, he was a constant threat for opposition defences. Whether running in behind, stretching the game or creating opportunities for those around him, Charlie's selfless play was just as important as his goals.

His ability to bring teammates into the game and provide crucial assists made him one of the driving forces behind our attacking success.

Defensive Record Award

Luke Munns

Every successful team needs a goalkeeper they can rely on.

For Sue's Angels FC, that goalkeeper was Luke Munns.

Time and time again Luke delivered when called upon, providing the calmness, leadership and match-winning saves that every championship-winning side requires. His performances throughout the campaign helped the club achieve a historic defensive record and finish the season with one of the league's strongest backlines.

A fantastic season between the sticks and a fully deserved award.

Goal of the Season

Malachi Mullings

Some goals win games.

Some goals leave people speechless.

Malachi's stunning strike against Mala Vida falls firmly into the second category.

A moment of pure quality and a goal that perfectly captured the confidence and talent he brings to the side. Malachi played a huge role in the club's success before his season was unfortunately cut short through injury.

Everyone at Sue's Angels FC wishes him a full recovery and looks forward to seeing him back in yellow for the 2026/27 campaign.

Top Goalscorer

Fraizer Isaias Osunkoya

Goals. Goals. And more goals.

Fraizer has the rare ability to change a game in an instant. One moment of quality, one touch, one finish, and suddenly the result looks very different.

His movement, composure and eye for goal made him one of the league's most dangerous forwards and a nightmare for defenders throughout the season.

When the team needed a goal, Fraizer was usually the man delivering it.

Clubman of the Year

Jimi El Bayati

Some people leave a mark that goes far beyond football.

Jimi has been a leader, a role model and a fantastic servant to Sue's Angels FC.

As he brings his playing career to a close, he does so having helped build the culture and standards that have driven the club forward. His commitment, positivity and support for everyone around him have made him an invaluable part of the journey.

A champion on the pitch and an even better person off it.

A true club legend.

Players' Player of the Year

Daniel McLane

If you combined the determination of Nemanja Vidic with the leadership of John Terry, you'd get something close to Daniel McLane.

Voted for by his teammates, Dan's impact this season cannot be overstated.

Defensively, he was as solid as a rock. In the opposition box, he became a genuine weapon from set pieces, producing crucial goals at key moments throughout the campaign.

A warrior, a leader and a player who set standards every single week.

Manager's Player of the Year

Dean Knight

Selected by the management team, Dean Knight's award is recognition of a season built on consistency, versatility and continuous improvement.

Week after week Dean grew into his role and became one of the team's most dependable performers.

Whether playing at right-back, right wing-back or even stepping into goal when required, Dean always delivered. His willingness to do whatever was needed for the team embodied everything Sue's Angels FC stands for.

With two clean sheets to his name as an emergency goalkeeper and outstanding performances across multiple positions, Dean's contribution to the undefeated season was immense.

While these awards recognise individual achievements, this season belongs to everyone.

The players. The coaching staff. The volunteers. The supporters. The sponsors. The families who backed the team every step of the way.

Together, we achieved something special.

An undefeated season.

Division Ten Champions.

A place in Sue's Angels FC history forever.

Champions. Invincibles. Together.
