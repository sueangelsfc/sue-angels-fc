William Clark Announces Retirement from Football

5 June 2026 · 1 min read · Sue's Angels FC

Sue’s Angels FC can confirm that William Clark has announced his retirement from football.

William played an important role during the club’s historic debut season, helping the Angels secure the League Ten title and complete an unbeaten league campaign.

Across 17 appearances, William contributed 7 goals and 8 assists, often making an impact from the bench and proving to be a valuable part of the squad throughout the season.

Everyone at Sue’s Angels FC would like to thank William for his commitment, contribution and support during his time with the club.

We wish him all the very best for the future and congratulate him on leaving the game as a league champion.

Thank you, William
