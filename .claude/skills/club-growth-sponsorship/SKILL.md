# Club Growth & Sponsorship

Turn anonymous visitors into supporters, players and sponsors — ethically and durably.

## The honest truth about "capturing visitors"
You **cannot** identify an anonymous individual visitor — not technically, not legally (GDPR/PECR). Growth is about giving people a **reason to identify themselves**, then re-reaching them with **consented** retargeting. Anyone promising automatic visitor emails is selling something illegal or fake.

## Capture (never lose a lead)
- **Every enquiry form writes to your database first** (a private, admin-read table), with `mailto` only as a fallback — a form that just opens an email app loses anyone who never hits send.
- **Newsletter sign-ups** go to a write-only supporters table + your email platform.
- **Lead magnets**: gate the **sponsorship pack** PDF behind a business email; offer "matchday updates" for supporter emails. Trade value for contact details.
- Clear, single CTAs everywhere: Join, Sponsor, Follow, Subscribe.

## Sponsorship funnel
- A sponsors page that sells the *cause and the reach*, not just logos. Tailored packages (kit, signage, content, community), not rigid tiers.
- **B2B visitor intelligence** (Leadfeeder/Dealfront) can reveal which *companies* (by network IP) viewed the sponsors page — legitimate for outreach; it identifies organisations, never individuals.

## Newsletter & retargeting
- Automate a **monthly newsletter** (see **newsletter-manager**): build a branded PDF/email from live news, publish, and send to consented supporters.
- **Retargeting** matches the network to where ads run: reuse existing **GA4** for Google/YouTube (no new pixel — link GA4 to Google Ads); Meta/TikTok/LinkedIn each need their tag. Everything loads **only behind a cookie-consent banner**. Setup is free; you only pay for ad spend you choose.

## Rules
Minimal, consented, privacy-preserving data. Never put PII in URLs. Dispatch **growth-manager** and **newsletter-manager**. Part of **football-website-builder**.
