Jim's Playing Career Comes to an End, But His Sue's Angels Journey Continues

05 Jun 2026 Sue's Angels FC

Sue's Angels FC bid an emotional farewell to club captain Jim on Sunday as he made the final playing appearance of his football career.

Sue's Angels FC bid an emotional farewell to club captain Jim on Sunday as he made the final playing appearance of his football career.

While the occasion marked the end of an era on the pitch, it also provided an opportunity to celebrate the impact one individual has had on the club during its historic first season.

Since the formation of Sue's Angels FC, Jim has been much more than a captain. He has been a leader, a role model and one of the driving forces behind everything the team has achieved.

At the heart of a defence that helped deliver an unbeaten league campaign, Jim's performances throughout the season have been consistently outstanding. Week after week, he led by example, setting standards both on and off the pitch and earning the respect of teammates, opponents and everyone connected with the club.

It seemed only fitting that his final appearance ended in typical fashion. A commanding display, a Man of the Match performance and another clean sheet helped complete a season that will be remembered for years to come.

For manager Stephen Epathite, the moment was particularly emotional.

"Jim is my friend, my brother and someone I respect completely as both a man and a footballer. Everything he has given to this club has been done with honesty, commitment and pride. He has all my love and respect."

While Sue's Angels will undoubtedly miss seeing their captain cross the white line each Sunday, there is good news for everyone associated with the club.

Jim's playing days may be over, but his involvement with Sue's Angels FC is far from finished. He will remain part of the club and join the coaching staff on the sidelines next season as the Angels prepare for life in League Eight.

As one chapter closes, another begins.

Thank you for everything, Jim.

A captain. A leader. A friend. A Sue's Angels legend.
