Club honours.

First season, first silverware. League Ten won at the first attempt, and promotion sealed as champions.

The season →

League Ten Champions

25/26 · Champions

Champions of League Ten at the first attempt, unbeaten across the whole season. Played eighteen, won eighteen, twenty-one points clear at the top.

Promoted to League Eight

25/26 · Promoted

Sealed promotion as champions, stepping up to League Eight for the 26/27 season. The reward for a perfect campaign, and the next mountain to climb.

25/26 · Awards night

The winners.

Announced after the title was won. Some chosen by the manager, some voted for by the squad, all earned across an unbeaten year.

01

Top Goalscorer

Frazier-Isaías Osunkoya

No. 30 Forward

Goals, goals and more goals. Movement, composure and a finish in him at any moment. When the team needed one, Frazier usually delivered.

02

Top Assister

Charlie Dunkley

No. 9 Midfield engine

Relentless all season. Energy, intelligence and selfless running made him a constant threat, his assists and link play driving our attack.

03

Players' Player of the Year

Daniel McLane

No. 25 Voted by the squad

Voted for by his teammates. Solid as a rock in defence and a real weapon from set pieces. A warrior who set the standard every single week.

04

Manager's Player of the Year

Dean Knight

No. 20 Manager's pick

Consistency, versatility and constant improvement. Right-back, wing-back, even emergency keeper with two clean sheets. Whatever the team needed, Dean delivered.

05

Defensive Record Award

Luke Munns

No. 28 Goalkeeper

The goalkeeper every championship side needs. Calm, commanding and full of match-winning saves as the Angels set a historic defensive record.

06

Goal of the Season

Malachi Mullings

No. 27 The strike vs Mala Vida

A stunning strike against Mala Vida, pure quality. A huge part of the season before injury cut it short. We can't wait to see him back.

07

Clubman of the Year

Jim El Bayati

No. 10 Club servant

A leader, role model and true club servant. As he closes his playing career he leaves the culture and standards that drive the club. A true legend.

Leadership

The armband.

The first captaincy group in the club's history led the side through the inaugural, unbeaten campaign.

C

Club Captain

Jim El Bayati

The first club captain in Sue's Angels FC history, leading the side through the inaugural 25/26 season and the League Ten title.

Vice-Captain

Daniel McLane

A warrior in defence and the squad's own Players' Player of the Year. The captain's deputy, and a standard-setter every week.

Third-Choice Captain

Andrew Allen

Completing the leadership group that steered an unbeaten season, ready to wear the armband whenever the team called on him.
