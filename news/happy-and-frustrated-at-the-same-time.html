Interview

Happy and frustrated at the same time

25 October 2026 · 4 min read · Sue's Angels FC

Interview3:11 Happy and frustrated at the same time 25 Oct 2026Sue’s Angels FC

Sue's Angels went out of the Supreme Trophies Marcus Lipton Cup on Sunday morning, beaten 4-3 on penalties by Juizo FC at Clapham Common after ninety goalless minutes against a side four divisions above us. Luke Munns kept the clean sheet his manager had asked for. The goal at the other end never came.

Afterwards we spoke to Stephen Epathite.

Press play above for the whole conversation. Everything he said is written out below it, and every timestamp takes you straight to that answer in the recording.

A goal. A goal. One-nil would have done it.Hear him say it 1:00

0:07A disappointing way to exit the competition, particularly after holding a side four divisions above us to a goalless draw. What was your overall assessment of today's performance?

I am happy and I am frustrated at the same time.

Defensively, the whole team were very disciplined. Off the ball they were disciplined and they executed what I wanted from the start, which was a clean sheet, which was to keep them out, which was to frustrate them, which was to make them play long balls over the top, which I knew that Luke and the players in front of him could deal with.

And the plan genuinely was to win the game one or two nil. We executed half of that plan and not the other half.

0:49We came into today's game having scored 24 goals in five games, but for one reason or another we could not find a breakthrough this time. What do you feel was missing from our attacking play today?

A goal. A goal. One-nil would have done it. We were comfortable defensively.

Luke pulled off an outstanding save, which he usually does, but I thought we were comfortable defensively.

We need to make sure that we are scoring goals in important situations. Set pieces, open play, chances that we get. That is what was missing today, just a goal.

1:25After ninety minutes of hard work, to go out on penalties is obviously difficult to take. What was your message to the boys after the game?

It is a shit one, going out on penalties.

The message at the end was pretty much what I said about being frustrated that we could not win the game in ninety minutes, which I thought we were good for. Obviously we did not score the goal, so we were not. But I think a lot of the players felt frustrated that we did not win the game in ninety minutes.

And the message was that I was proud of them. When a team executes something that you want as a manager or a coach, you always feel happy, and you always feel happy that they have listened and taken the responsibility to do that, which I was really happy with.

As I said, it was just the goal that was missing.

2:18Finally, we have got plenty to play for this season, including another cup tie against Celtic Swans, which has already been confirmed, but we are just waiting for a date. How do we make sure we take lessons from today and come back stronger?

The lessons from today would be that defensively I thought we were fine. We had enough firepower through the back and the middle to keep clean sheets and keep teams out.

But we need to find other ways of scoring if our strikers on the day cannot find that goal. We need to find goals from set pieces, which we did last year. We found goals from set pieces from different players, which was very effective.

So the message will be the same. It will be that we have to be hard to beat, and when we get a chance in front of goal we have to take it. That will be the difference.

What is left

The Brian Howard President's Cup tie at home to Celtic Swans FC is confirmed, with the date still to be set.

League Eight returns on Sunday 1 November, at The Reeves Sports Club against TSM Rovers, 11:00.
