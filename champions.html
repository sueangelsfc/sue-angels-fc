What it means

More than a perfect record.

Eighteen games, eighteen wins, and promotion out of League Ten at the first time of asking. On paper it is as clean as a season gets. But the number that matters most is not on the table.

Every one of those wins was played in the name of Susan Anne Martin, and for every family who has lost someone to sepsis. The trophy sits in a cabinet. The reason we chased it does not. That is the record we are proudest of, and it is the one we carry into next season.

What we do in life echoes in eternity.
