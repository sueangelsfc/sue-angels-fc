03The report Written by the club

The full report.

Angels strike four to secure second-round spot

A stunning opener from Ade and two goals in quick succession after half-time helped us secure a 4–0 win at Pep’s All Stars and a place in the second round of the Brian Howard President’s Cup.

Ade finished the afternoon with two goals, with LJ and Dan McLane also finding the net as we returned from Dulwich with a clean sheet and our cup campaign underway.

Six minutes, and the trip was worth it

It took just six minutes for us to break the deadlock, and the opener was worth the trip alone. Andy’s cushioned header found Ade outside the area, where he produced a beautifully controlled volley to lob the goalkeeper and put us ahead.

The second arrived in the 28th minute. Sebastian launched a Rory Delap-esque throw into the penalty area, and LJ met it with a glancing header to double our lead.

There could have been more before the break. Both Charlie and Ade struck the crossbar, and on another afternoon we might have gone into half-time four goals ahead. Pep’s still offered a threat on the counter, though, providing a reminder that there was work left to do.

Manage it, and finish it off

At 2–0, Stephen’s half-time message was clear: manage the game more efficiently and finish it off.

Pep’s returned with renewed energy and started the second half brightly, catching us a little cold as they looked for a way back into the tie. Our response, however, came quickly.

A break forward earned our first corner of the half, and Dean delivered a beautiful ball onto Dan McLane’s head. Having scored regularly from corners last season, Dan showed that familiar threat again, heading home in the 49th minute to give us breathing space. On the balance of play, the three-goal advantage was a fair reflection of the chances we had created.

The best move of the afternoon

Just two minutes later, we added a fourth through one of the best moves of the afternoon.

Starting with Andy’s throw inside our own half, LJ flicked the ball on to Charlie, whose clever touch released Ade making a third-man run from just beyond halfway. Through one-on-one, Ade remained cool, calm and composed, rounding the goalkeeper before tapping into the empty net with his right foot.

It was a goal that combined sharp thinking, well-timed movement and an assured finish, putting us firmly in control of the tie.

Seeing it out

Further chances followed. Charlie struck the post to complete a frustrating afternoon in front of goal, having already hit the crossbar before the break. Substitute Jon Lloyd also came close with an ambitious effort from around 40 yards after spotting the opposition goalkeeper stranded out of position.

We saw out the remaining minutes comfortably, protecting our clean sheet and securing a place in round two against Celtic Swans.

Next up

Our next League Eight match was to have been at home to Mousa Mountain on Sunday 18 October. It will not be played. Mousa Mountain have folded, and the fixture has been called off. It is never good news when a club goes under and we wish everybody there well.

That leaves the President's Cup second round at home to Celtic Swans as the next fixture in the diary. The date is still to be confirmed.

Thank you to Pep’s All Stars for hosting us. We wish them all the best for the rest of the season.
