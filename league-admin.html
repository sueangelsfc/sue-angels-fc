Update the League section (table, results and leading scorers) for a new season without touching any code. Paste the data from FA Full-Time, check the preview, and save. This is the FA division data only, and is kept separate from the club's own match entries. Built-in season data is used until you save an override here.

Sign in with your admin account to edit league data.

League table

One club per line, in finishing order. Separate columns with a pipe | (or a tab/comma). Format:
Club | P | W | D | L | GF | GA | Pts   e.g.  Sue's Angels FC | 18 | 18 | 0 | 0 | 90 | 11 | 54
Goal difference and position are worked out automatically.

Around the league (results)

One match per line, most recent first. Format:
Date | Home | Score | Away   e.g.  31 May 26 | Sue's Angels FC | 2-0 | Hillside Elite FC Blues

Leading scorers

One player per line, in order. Format:
Player | Club | Goals | Assists | Apps   e.g.  Frazier-Isaías Osunkoya | Sue's Angels FC | 25 | 18 | 19
Fill in both lists if your league records them; otherwise just fill "All competitions".

Tip: use Load current to pull the existing data into the box, edit it, then Save. Reset to default removes your override and restores the built-in data. Changes appear on the League page on its next load.
