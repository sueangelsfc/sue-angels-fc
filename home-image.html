Our cause · In her name

"What we do in life echoes in eternity."

Sue's Angels FC was founded in memory of Susan Anne Martin, who we lost to sepsis. We play for her, and we talk openly about sepsis so that fewer families have to go through the same. Know the signs — it can save a life.

Know the signs →
Slurred speechConfusion or drowsiness
Extreme shiveringFever or severe muscle pain
Passing no urineIn a day
Severe breathlessnessOr breathing very fast
"I feel like I might die"A deep sense something is wrong
Mottled or pale skinBlotchy or discoloured
